# ShuttlePro POS & Management System Plan

The ShuttlePro POS & Management System is designed to bridge the gap between hardware-heavy transit systems and agile, software-defined operations.

## Phase 1: Terminal Operations (Current)

- **POS Interface:** Quick-tap boarding and subscription sales.
- **Fleet Tracking:** Real-time GPS broadcasting.
- **User Lifecycle:** Bulk enrollment and custom billing tiers (Daily, Weekly, Custom).

## Phase 2: Operational Intelligence (Added in this update)

- **Revenue Auditing:** Real-time tracking of subscription vs. cash-equivalent revenue.
- **Occupancy Monitoring:** Tracking passenger load factors.
- **Maintenance Logs:** Scheduling vehicle health checks.

## Phase 3: Rider Experience (Roadmap)

- **Rider App:** A lightweight PWA for riders to see live ETAs.
- **QR Ticketing:** Generating digital passes for mobile scanning.
- **Push Alerts:** Notifying riders of delays or route changes.

## Key Additions & Concepts for Future Growth

- **Analytics Dashboard (Live Now):** A high-level view for management that tracks gross revenue, subscription vs. cash-equivalent splits, and fleet efficiency.
- **Shuttle Telemetry (Live Now):** The Dispatch view simulates real-time vehicle health, including speed, fuel levels, and operator fatigue sensors.
- **Command Center Aesthetics:** The UI uses an enterprise transit style with heavy blacks, high-contrast blues, and an operations-first language model.
- **Operational Matrix:** The schedule is presented as a weekly matrix to help managers identify coverage gaps quickly.

## Proposed Features (Implementation Ideas)

- **Automated Billing:** Integration with a real payment processor for card charges.
- **Route Heatmaps:** Visualization of high-boarding locations to optimize routing.
- **Shift Overlap Alerts:** Warnings for dispatchers when two drivers are scheduled for the same shuttle.
- **Inventory Tracking:** Monitoring shuttle cleaning supplies or onboard consumables.
